The Spring 2025 archive is the richest legacy set in the repository. It includes a structured introductory week, a teleportation reference sheet, and a multi-module algorithm workshop sequence.

## Week 1 Material

The Week 1 lesson plan covers:

- What quantum computing is and why it matters
- Qubits and their vector representation
- Bra-ket notation
- Inner and outer products
- Tensor products for multi-qubit systems
- Pauli-$X$ and identity gates

This older plan lines up closely with the later Fundamentals sequence, which makes it useful as a historical precursor.

## Week 4 Teleportation Material

The archived teleportation reference sheet is unusually complete. It covers:

- Required resources: one shared Bell pair plus two classical bits
- Alice's CNOT, Hadamard, and measurement steps
- Bob's conditional correction logic
- No-cloning and no-faster-than-light communication constraints
- A compact mathematical derivation of the protocol

### Download

- [Teleportation reference sheet](/resources/archive/spring-2025/week4/teleportation-reference.pdf)

## Algorithm Workshop Modules

The Spring 2025 algorithm workshop material is organized as a progression:

1. Fundamentals and operations
2. Teleportation
3. Deutsch's algorithm
4. Shor's algorithm

There are also standalone slide decks on:

- Single systems
- Multiple systems
- Quantum circuits
- Entanglement in action
- Phase estimation and factoring
